Dear Emory,  In our book of Tombstone Inscriptions, p.151, there is a listing of Kincaid cemetery, there are 8 people buried there. 3 by the name of Kincaid, Dora A.; an infant daughter; and Johnny Kincaid. Children of AH and EA Kincaid. It is located in Guelph Township, R1E, T34, Sec. 26, NW corner of NE 80.  Located from Portland: 2 E, 1/4 S.  The land belonged to Mr. Lorton, first burial was thought to be in 1870. Mr. Lorton died when the children were very young, and his wife remarried A H Kincaid.  They had 2 daughters die of Black diptheria and were the first burials in the cemetery.  There were 2 other Kincaide children: Henry and Cora.
information from the Scoop.
Hope this helps.  the cemetery is no longer there
Jane Moore
volunteer
